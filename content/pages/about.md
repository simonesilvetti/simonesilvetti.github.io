I am a postdoctoral fellow at the Department of Engineering and Architecture of the University of Trieste. I studied mathematics in Rome, then moved to Trieste, where I spent ten years at [Esteco](https://www.esteco.com) working on numerical methods and machine learning, while doing a PhD in computer science at the University of Udine.

My research combines machine learning and quantitative formal methods to verify and design complex systems, mainly cyber-physical systems.

More on what I work on is on the [research](/research) page; the full list of publications is on the [papers](/papers) page and on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=q_ax9IUAAAAJ).

## Experience

- **July 2024 – now**: Postdoctoral fellow (assegno di ricerca), Department of Engineering and Architecture, University of Trieste.
- **April 2023 – July 2024**: Researcher and software developer in the Research and Development Group at Esteco, working on process mining, verification and simulation of autonomous driving systems, and data analysis.
- **June 2014 – March 2023**: Researcher and software developer in the Numerical Methods Group at Esteco, looking after the mathematical algorithms of the company's software.
- **June 2013 – May 2014**: Area Science Park internship at Esteco. I worked on Support Vector Machines for classification and regression, and the code was then included in Esteco's products.
