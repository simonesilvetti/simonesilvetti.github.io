## The problem

Parameter verification and synthesis for stochastic systems are still hard problems, and they become even harder for large-scale systems when the goal is the satisfaction of linear temporal logic formulae.

## This talk

An invited talk at SynCoP 2019, a workshop of ETAPS held in Prague on 6–7 April 2019, with a short accompanying abstract (the workshop had no formal proceedings). It gives a review of a number of techniques designed to tackle these problems. They rely on **Gaussian Process (GP) regression** and an efficient **Bayesian optimisation** algorithm.

Related work: [Bayesian statistical parameter synthesis](/papers/bayesian-statistical-parameter-synthesis) and the [Winter Simulation Conference tutorial](/papers/bayesian-parametric-verification-synthesis-machine-learning).
